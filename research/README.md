# Historical research, not validated results

These files preserve the user's November 2026 research and supplied Taiwan resource review. Many statements conflict or are incompletely verified. Treat citations as leads, not proof of dataset availability, license scope or best-model claims.

Corrections guiding this repository:

1. Multimodal Raman/image learning is not impossible in principle; it needs appropriate paired evidence and modality-specific encoders. No validated paired Taiwan set was established here.
2. Cleanup tonnage and beach surveys are not direct ground truth for floating-debris segmentation; effort, time and spatial support differ.
3. Government site lists are weak labels. Waste visibility, clearance date and legality require distinct definitions.
4. MOENV LDate is last update, not listing/event date. API wr_p_244 returns archive metadata, not site rows.
5. No blanket training or latency claim follows from model size. Record actual measurements.
6. A report search finding no open Taiwan spectra or paper does not prove none exists.
7. License badges, code licenses and article licenses do not necessarily cover underlying datasets or weights.
8. Raw spectral modalities cannot be interchanged just because both are arrays. USGS reflectance is not Raman.
9. Archived scripts/paths and dates describe earlier sessions and are not commands to execute.
